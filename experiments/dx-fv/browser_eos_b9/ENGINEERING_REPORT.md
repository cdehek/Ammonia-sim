## Ownership and failure behavior

The worker is the sole authoritative physical-state owner. Main owns rendering,
input, request tracking, errors and worker lifecycle. A service scheduling stop
never fabricates a physical model stop. Synchronous advance cannot be interrupted
safely in Newton; stop/dispose waits until the requested advance completes.
No partial original trial is published. Duplicate request IDs replay rather than
re-execute. Session/sequence/version checks prevent stale display installation.
A hard termination destroys a disposable session and leaves in-flight state
unknown; only the last received accepted snapshot is known.

## Measured engineering tradeoff

Final physics-phase main/worker maximum heartbeat gaps are **196.8/30.3 ms in
Chromium** and **187/39 ms in WebKit**. Their physics-phase p95 gaps are
**196.1/18.2 ms** and **181/15 ms** respectively. Whole measured-phase maxima,
including diagnostic transport, are **196.8/58.6 ms** and **187/39 ms**.
These are B9 whole-model measurements, not B7 historical property-only numbers.

Worker startup/new-session medians are **41.5/55.5 ms** versus main **21.6/20 ms**
(Chromium/WebKit). Worker throughput is generally lower in these short startup
workloads; the tradeoff buys responsiveness, not faster physics. Median real
button automation roundtrip improves to about **2.29/4.62 ms**, from about
**43.30/47.78 ms**, but worker input maxima still reach **210.82/158.26 ms**.
Desktop WebKit retains frame gaps up to **330 ms main / 354 ms worker**. Do not call this
perfectly smooth or suitable for real-time plant operation.

Memory observations are bounded and coarse. Table physics has no WASM memory.
Summed browser-child RSS includes shared pages and does not isolate a worker.
Chromium and WebKit reach roughly 475/490 MiB after the third worker disposal,
after earlier main sessions; no leak or leak-freedom conclusion follows. Full
research traces/caches add copying cost. Cache references are cleared on disposal;
worker termination and original native deletion are explicitly exercised.
