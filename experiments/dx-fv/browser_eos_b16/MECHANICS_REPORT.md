# Frozen optimization mechanics

B16 changes no scientific function. Control and candidate are byte-identical copies of B15's sealed final sources. The candidate carries a fresh private holder within one finite primitive numeric vapor PH inversion. The same lexical pressure and frozen table make its pressure index/weight reusable during that inversion. Ownership ends at return/throw. B9C boundary cache ownership remains independent and unchanged.

Original vapor PH performs initial saturation, initial vapor branch support check, 38 bisections, final saturation and final branch interpolation. Candidate preserves all 40 branch interpolations, bisections, arithmetic, table interpolation, density logs, support checks and phase decisions. Only repeated pressure bracket lookup is reused: 42 → 3. Successful eligible calls remove 39 source bracket-array constructions and add one holder construction. These are source sites, not measured allocations; V8 may eliminate or move allocations.

The candidate adds the optional owned argument to internal branch/PT helpers and holder-selection logic to eligible inversion. Public PT forwards only original p/T/kind, preserving arity 3 and ignoring a fourth public argument. The forwarding closure is created once per engine; wrapper invocation overhead may apply to public PT calls. Two-phase PH exits before holder creation. Other branch paths still encounter altered helper signatures/code shape. Their machine-code/inlining/GC consequences cannot be inferred from source alone.

Cold has far fewer eligible vapor inversions than warm. This makes overhead relatively more important as a hypothesis, but reduced bracket work does not by itself establish solver timing or the cause of cold regressions. B16's separate context counts, counters-only timing, warmed JIT, no-optimizing-JIT sensitivity and trace receipts characterize this question. No proposed improvement is implemented. Any wrapper/holder/code-shape redesign belongs in a separately authorized future milestone with fresh exact science gates.

Five finite M/U/V inventories, 21 coordinates, HEM assumptions, signed flux/donor enthalpy, eleven thermal storage states, Newton/signed finite differences, TR-BDF2, adaptive decisions, support bounds and strict rollback remain original. All accepted-source and table bytes remain frozen. Bulk wet is not proof of compressor carryover or an equipment safety trip.
