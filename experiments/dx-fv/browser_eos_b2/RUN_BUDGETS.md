# Execution scope and resource budgets

This executor has a cgroup allowance of two CPUs and 8 GiB RAM. Scientific runs
can share those resources; their elapsed times are not controlled speed comparisons.
Primary browser operating groups include three full accepted tolerance regimes
and the additional accepted maxStep=.025 refinement; they are substantially
larger than B1's individual operating runs.

Budget decisions recorded during B2: allow up to 30 minutes per browser operating
group, a15min soft target per remaining scientific contract block, and90min for the
primary browser assertion campaign. Allow 10 minutes per standalone backend
matrix and 20 minutes for the matched performance/offline campaign after other
numerical workers have stopped. Partial/failed evidence must survive a cutoff.
These wall budgets include scheduler contention and are resource limits, not
scientific acceptance thresholds. No tolerance or step probe changes on timeout.

in progress, after approximately 850 seconds of resource-sharing execution, to
prioritize directly applicable browser runs. It remains INCOMPLETE. Node's
complete nominal controls suite finished separately and is not that incomplete
run. The accepted table baseline ran at reduced OS scheduling priority; its
elapsed times must not be compared with browser or B1 timings.
