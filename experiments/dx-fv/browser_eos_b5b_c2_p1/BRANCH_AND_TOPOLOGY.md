is unique locally. The two endpoint coexistence root exists and is locally

Endpoint densities are positive and separated, and cv, dp/drho, cp and

The local Q signs do not establish exclusion of all mixtures at other
temperatures, other endpoint roots or other relevant thermodynamic states.

## Bounded stopping decision

No continuous density/temperature tiling, supporting-tangent inequality over a
full operating rectangle, or exhaustive competing-root exclusion was attempted.
The measured root tubes are roughly 1e-24 kg/m³ in endpoint density and
1e-26 K for mixture T; they are not coverage of a whole 35-bar operating region.
All sampled homogeneous temperatures are about 335–365 K, mixture temperatures
about 334.64–347.90 K. Those observed ranges are not a proved global search
domain. Critical behavior, solid phases and remote mathematical EOS branches
are not investigated or silently ruled out.

The stopping condition is the essential unproved topology/convex-envelope
obligation, not an arithmetic precision failure. More digits would not establish
that theorem. This experiment therefore stops with rigorous partial results;
it does not assert mathematical impossibility.

The smallest future mathematical step would be an independent review that
defines a physically relevant bounded domain and supplies or assesses one
explicit supporting-tangent/topology theorem for this pinned EOS. That is a
separate research authorization. Until then, park further certification/porting
and prioritize the separately scoped rejected-PT loader repair. Neither begins
here. The requested fully stable positive controls also remain an open gate;
their local positive inequalities cannot be relabeled global stability.
