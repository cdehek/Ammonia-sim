What changes when you adjust suction pressure?

Lower suction pressure lowers the evaporating temperature and increases the compression ratio when discharge pressure stays fixed.

The cycle’s energy balance

The evaporator absorbs heat, the compressor adds work, and the condenser rejects both to the surroundings. Lower evaporating temperature enables colder cooling, but generally makes compression more demanding.

Heat rejected = heat absorbed + work input

This simulator explores pressure and temperature. Cooling capacity, refrigerant flow, compressor power and room temperature require additional equipment and load data.
