A bounded, traceable physics model

Solver version 0.2.0

Properties are interpolated from a grid generated using CoolProp 7.2.0, HEOS::Ammonia, using the Gao–Wu–Bell–Lemmon equation of state (2020). No ideal-gas compressor approximation is used. The HTML embeds its property grid and works offline. State requests outside its domain are rejected.

These are maximum observed numerical errors in the tested cases, not total physical uncertainty or a guarantee for every possible operating condition. Reference cycles use direct CoolProp; the manufacturer check uses a separate REFPROP-derived controller correlation. Neither replaces measurements from an actual plant.

Equations & accounting boundaries

h₂s = h(P₂, s₁)
h₂ = h₁ + (h₂s − h₁) / ηᵢₛ
h₄ = h₃ (isenthalpic expansion)
Q̇ₑ = ṁ(h₅ − h₄)
Ẇfluid = ṁ(h₂ − h₁)
Ẇelectrical = Ẇfluid / ηmotor
Q̇line = ṁ(h₁ − h₅)
Q̇cond = Q̇ₑ + Q̇line + Ẇfluid

Motor losses are shown separately and are assumed to enter the surroundings, not the refrigerant. Evaporator superheat contributes to cooling duty; suction-line heating does not. Expansion quality is a vapor mass fraction.

Operating scope & limits

  • Compressor suction: 0.35–8 bar absolute. Condensing: 3–30 bar absolute, above evaporator pressure and cooling-air temperature.
  • Property grid: 0.3–35 bar absolute; vapor superheat ≤ 250 K; liquid subcooling ≤ 30 K; minimum liquid temperature −73.15 °C.
  • Dry compression, fixed efficiencies, steady flow and one refrigerant. Liquid-line flash before the valve is rejected.
  • Flooded mode is an ideal separator boundary. Pump head, overfeed ratio and vessel inventory are not calculated.
  • UA checks and compressor limits are screening assumptions. No manufacturer rating map is included.
  • Two-stage intercooling and refrigerant-inventory dynamics need separately validated models and equipment inputs.

Gauge pressures use 1.01325 bar atmosphere. Pressure losses use pressure differences without an atmospheric offset. Temperature differences use K or °F differences.
